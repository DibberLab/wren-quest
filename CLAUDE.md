# Wren Quest

An 8-bit top-down action-adventure game (original IP: hero Wren, the Ember Crown, the Obsidian King). Static site.

- Hostname: https://quest.dibberlab.me
- dibberlab-infra site name: `wren-quest` (`server/sites/wren-quest.conf`, TYPE=static, standard static nginx template)
- Served from: `site/` (the whole game is `site/index.html`)

## Working on this

- One file on purpose: HTML, CSS and JS inline in `site/index.html`. No build step, no npm. Fonts come from Google Fonts (Press Start 2P, Silkscreen).
- The game is a 256x232 canvas: 56px HUD on top, 256x176 playfield (16x11 tiles of 16px). Fixed 60 fps tick in `tick()`, rendering in `draw()`.
- World generation is seeded (`SEED`), so the map is the same for every player. Changing `SEED`, `WW`/`WH`, `CEN`, or the cave pattern reshuffles the world (fine pre-launch; your own test save may then point at odd places, so start a new game).
- **Pre-launch: there are no players yet.** Build every change as if the game hasn't launched: no save migrations, no handling for saves made before a change, no "if a player is already at X" cases. Changing the world or rules freely is fine.
- Saves live in localStorage under `SKEY` (`wrenquest-save-v3`). New state fields go in `newState()`; loading does `Object.assign(newState(), save)`.
- Music and sound effects are synthesized with Web Audio (`SONGS`, `sfx()`). Keep the music original; do not reproduce melodies from existing games. Song flags in `buildSong()`/`playEv()`: `doom` (filtered detuned-saw drone on each chord root, `drone()`), `gal` (galloping pulse bass replacing the octave bass), `pow` (a fifth under the lead), plus `arp`, `pad`, `dbl`. Drum letters: `k` kick, `K` heavy kick, `s` snare, `h` hat, `t`/`T` low/high tom, `c` crash. The title, overworld, dungeon, boss, duel and cavern themes are minor/modal and "epic + doom" by request; each lead bar is 16 steps and there is one chord bar per 16 steps.
- Disc golf: `HOLES`/`HOLEOF`/`BASKETS` define the 27-hole course (seeded, one per region in `CEN` plus 3, numbered by distance from `START`). Strokes on the map are counted per screen visit in `G.strokes`. See Hole dungeons and Golf challenge below for what sinking a basket does now. Every 9 holes `openCard()` shows the scorecard (`drawCard()`, stats from `progress()`). Cards are by hole number: card 1 is holes 1-9, card 2 is 10-18, card 3 is 19-27; unplayed holes show `-`. On the pause map page, sunk baskets are green and found-but-unsunk ones white; A opens the card with the next unplayed hole (`nextHole()`).
- Caverns: `CAVS` (17, built at load from `SEED+4242`) are blobs of screens on a 9x7 grid; `genCavScreen(cv,gx,gy)` makes a screen (room kind `'cav'`). Overworld entrances are `MOUTHAT` (tile `MO`, hidden as boulder `BB` or soil `DG` until opened, tracked in `S.opened` as `'m:'+key`); ladders `LD` lead back up. Buried treasure is `DIGAT`. `pickAction()` is the Pick-Shovel. Darkness is `drawDark()`. Cave-only enemies: `mole`, `spider`, `crab`, `wisp` (stats in `ENC`). Rewards taken: `S.cavr`; visited screens: `S.cvis`; dug spots: `S.dug`.
- Stamina: `P.st` (not saved), max `stMax()` = 100. `stOk()`/`stSpend(n)`/`stTick()` next to `updPlayer()`. Costs: roll 28, jump 24, sprint 0.55/frame (hold B after the roll), spin 34, axe 12, hammer 18, pick 6. At 0 Wren is `P.winded` until 40%. Jumping while sprinting keeps sprint speed (`P.airRun`).
- Monsters: stats in `EN` (overworld/dungeon) and `ENC` (cave-only), AI in `AI`, sprites in `SPR`, three tier palettes in `EPAL` (`e.pal`/`e.pk` override per entity, used by folk foes). Biome spawn lists are `BIO[*].en`; dungeon lists are `ENB`; cavern lists are each `CAVS[i].pool` (no `mole`, by request).
- Balance (dialled back on request): overworld foes per screen `n` in `genScreen()` (0-4), caverns spawn 60% of `rm.n`, dungeon rooms 65% of `rm.en` (trimmed at spawn so seeded layouts stay put). `dropAt()` drops something about a third of the time.
- Marebear goo: `gooAt(x,y,big)` adds a slick to `G.slicks` (cleared in `setRoom()`); `updGoo()`/`drawGoo()`/`onGoo()`. Standing in it sets `G.bf.sticky` (70% speed). Marebears drip small slicks while walking and leave a big one when killed or beaten in the charm duel.
- Charm duel: the first time a Marebear rears up, 30% of the time she becomes `G.duelPending='marebear'` (`CHAMPS.marebear`, moves `charm`/`swoon` fire `lheart` shots with `love`). Hits call `charmHit()` and fill `G.duel.love` (decays slowly; rolling, jumping and the shield avoid it). At 100 the duel is lost with `D.lovelost`, and `endDuel()` takes a heart container (`S.maxHp`-2, min 6) and counts it in `S.mareHearts` (saved). Winning a Marebear duel while `S.mareHearts>0` gives one back. While she holds a heart, smitten Marebears pick the duel 60% of the time (not 30%). Keep it cartoonish and all-ages.
- Mushrooms: only `giant`, `boom` and `frost` have `bag:1` and go in the pouch; the rest apply on pickup. `startLoaded()` clears a selected item that is no longer in `INV`.
- People of the land: `spawnFolk()` (22% of overworld screens without fixed NPCs) adds `G.npcs` entries with `wand:1` and a `kind`: talk, sage, trader, gift, thief, bandit, challenge (`FOLK_KIND` weights, `FOLK_TALK` lines). `folkTalk()` handles talking; `updFolk()` handles wandering, thieves walking up to steal and bandits ambushing. Hostile folk become `thief`/`bandit` enemies via `folkFoe()`. A fleeing thief that reaches an edge moves to the next screen (`G.thief`) up to 3 times, and killing him returns `e.loot`. Challengers and 35% of bandits call `startDuel()`.
- Recurring cast: `CAST` holds Kestrel (the cocky prodigy rival, 6 beats), Morrow (the King's herald, 3), Old Rowan (the groundskeeper, 3), Mittens (3, only after the cat quest) and Granny (3 chats at home after the cat quest). Each beat is one short conversation: `say(r)` (r = rapport) and 2-3 replies `[label, reaction, rapport delta, optional effect]`. A cast member appears by the basket of any hole not yet won once `pieces()` reaches the beat's `min`, one per screen in `CAST_ORDER` (`castDue()`, placed in `placeExtras()`), then leaves after the talk. `beatTalk(id)` plays a beat; `ask(text, [[label, fn]])` is the reply menu on the dialog box (up/down, A). State: `S.cast[id]={i,r}`. Tone: warm, a little odd, a little spooky, like the folk lines.
- Jump places: `jumpFeat()` runs at the end of `genScreen()` and is seeded with `h2(sx,sy,9191)`, so the world is unchanged. It skips screens with dungeons, caves, mouths, dig spots, baskets, NPCs, quests, arenas or the start. There are three kinds:
  - hurdles: `HU`, crossed only in mid-air; landing on one bounces Wren back
  - rune gates: `RP` pillars with an `RU` rune stone on each side; landing a jump on a rune sinks them, recorded in `S.opened` as `'r:'+key`
  - moat islands: a 1-wide ring (walking jump) or 3-wide ring (sprint jump) of water or lava around a chest; `'j:'+key` once opened
  A moat is only kept if every tile walkable from the first exit stays walkable.
  - disc gates: a seeded share of moat islands (`h2(sx,sy,9194)` against `TUNE.gates`: ember 0.3, crystal 0.25; 12 and 8 of the 39 today) swap the liquid ring for thorns `TH` (only a glowing disc, the Ember, burns them; `'e:'+key`) or seal stone `SG` with a crystal eye `EY` on the top edge (only the Crystal disc wakes it; `'c:'+key`). Same footprint as the moat, so connectivity is unchanged; jumps only clear `WA`/`LV`/`HU`, so these rings can't be hopped. `gateHit()` runs for quick and marker discs on the overworld, `gateOpen()` clears the ring; other discs get one hint per visit (`G.gateHint`).
- Unstick: `onEnter()` and `endDuel()` call `unstick()`. If Wren's tile can't be reached from the screen's exits (`reachFromExits()`, which allows jumps), Wren is moved to the nearest reachable free spot. Keep that call when adding new ways to place the player.
- Hole dungeons (by request the old 14 dungeons became 27 disc golf holes): the 14 old entrances (`DSCR` 1-14) are sealed ruins (`RK` where the door was); only the throne (`NDUN`=15) keeps its door. `DUN[HD0+n]` (`HD0`=100) is hole n's dungeon, built at load from the `DSPEC` theme matching the basket's biome, with `sz` (1-14, by hole number) standing in for the old dungeon number in `genDungeon()`. Holes 1-14 give the old dungeon items in order; odd holes have a heart container. Each hole has its own keeper from `HOLE_BOSS` (27 entries: name, sprite template `bA`/`bB`/`knight`, palette, attack patterns, minion), matched to its biome; the Bog Hydra is hole 3 and the Winter Queen (hole 27) gets +20 hp. `bossAI()` patterns: radial, aimed, spiral, charge, teleport, summon, shock, plus rain (drops fall from the top of the room), wall (a row with a three-shot gap), cross (four-way streams, diagonal when enraged), burrow (underground and unhittable via `inv`, too faint to touch, then erupts with a ring) and sweep (a fan that sweeps across Wren; mirrored when enraged). Side dungeons are still 20-28, so checks for them are `G.dn>=20&&G.dn<HD0`.
- Map throw: within `TUNE.golf.mapRange`=170 px of an unsunk map basket, with no foe within `mapFoeClear`=56 px and no disc out, the disc button opens the marker throw (`mtWanted()`, `startMT()`, `G.mode='mthrow'`, `G.mt`): the same marker, shape, power and release as the challenge, Q/E picks a disc, the item button backs out with no stroke. `mtThrow()` pushes a disc shot with a precomputed `pts` path (`golfPath`, no wind) that `updMDisc()` follows: walls and trees stop it (it lands as a `discdrop` to pick up), the chains catch it when it arrives below 85% of launch speed, a faster hit bounces off. The panel (`drawMT()`) sits at the top of the playfield when Wren is in the lower half. Elsewhere the disc is still a quick combat throw aimed by facing.
- Map baskets: `basketMade()` on a hole you haven't won sets `G.holeEnter`, and you drop into `DUN[HD0+n]` (`G.holeRet` remembers where you were; `exitDungeon()` returns there). Won holes just refill hearts.
- Golf challenge: when a hole boss dies, `updBossDie()` hands over to `startGolf()` (`G.mode='golf'`, `G.golf`). Holes are par 3 or 4 (no par 2s, by request). `golfCourse(h)` is seeded per hole: tee, basket, optional dogleg (`mid`) for par 4, trees, water, wind. Length is sized to the longest disc a player can have by that hole (`holeReach(n)`): 62 + 1.33x it for par 3, 62 + 2.09x for par 4, i.e. 341/501 ft with the Midrange (holes 1-2), longer from hole 3 when the Driver arrives. Water bands are kept off a dogleg's corner.
  - Biome courses: `COURSES` gives each of the 9 biomes a named course (EMBERGLEN GREEN, DEEPWOOD FAIRWAY, BOGWATER BENDS, GLASSWATER LINKS, CRAG RIDGE, SUNSCORCH DUNES, FROSTFANG FLATS, HOLLOW YARD, CINDER PIT) with its own colours, obstacle style (`drawObstacle`: tree, pine, snowpine, cypress, rock, cactus, tomb), hazard (`drawHazard`: water, bog, chasm, sand, ice, grave, lava; all play like water), hazard layout (`band`, `two`, `pools`, `pits`), tree density and wind factor. `holeBiome(h)` reads the basket screen's biome (cached in `h.bn`). Hole 1 is always a short, straight par 3 (`easy` in `golfCourse()`: basket centred, no dogleg, no water, few trees, light wind). The keeper's fall line names the course, and so does the intro card.
  - Discs: `TUNE.golf.discs` gives each disc its golf flight (max range, how much the shape bends, wind drift, Crystal's right turn and one tree glance). Keepers drop them (`TUNE.golf.discAt`): Midrange at hole 1, Driver 3, Crystal 6, Ember 9, so the bag is full by the end of the front nine. Shops no longer sell discs; the cavern Crystal became a gem stash. `golfBag()` lists the discs owned, `golfPick()` picks the shortest one that reaches at each new lie, Q/E changes it while aiming (`g.di`, `gDisc()`).
  - Field throw: arrows move the target marker `g.mk` (within the disc's range), B cycles the shot shape (`SHAPES`: hyzer/straight/anhyzer), A starts power (bar to 120%; 100% lands on the marker), A locks the release needle (`g.rel`: off-centre pulls/pushes the heading and adds curve). `golfAim()` solves the heading and distance so the chosen shape lands on the marker (no wind; the player allows for wind). The dotted preview is that path.
  - `golfFly()` simulates the flight: decel 0.05, curve grows as the disc slows, wind drift, trees block below height 13, the basket catches when close, low and slow. OB or water = +1 and replay from the last lie.
  - Putting mini-game: within `PUTT_FT`=30 ft, `startPutt()`/`updPutt()`/`drawPutt()` show a big basket with a left/right meter, then an up/down meter. The locked point must land inside the chains. The target shrinks and the needles speed up with distance. A miss leaves a short next putt, or a long one if it went over the top.
  - Par: as soon as strokes reach par without holing, the try fails (`g.ph='retry'`) and you replay from the tee. There's no boss refight. Every third failed try costs a heart container (`S.maxHp`-2, min 6), at most `TUNE.golf.heartsPerHole`=1 per hole ever (`S.golfLost[n]`, `golfHeartsLeft()`). B at the intro or the retry screen walks away (`golfLeave()`): back to the surface, `S.dun[d].bossDown` stays set, and entering the boss room again resumes the challenge (`G.golfWait`) with no refight.
  - Success (`golfFinish()`) awards the crown piece (`S.shards` holds `HD0+n`), records `S.bk` (the scorecard records challenge scores), unlocks the stairs and gives a heart piece if under par. `NSH`=27 pieces open the throne.
  - Camera: `g.z`/`g.cy` zoom out to show the whole hole in the intro and keep Wren and the marker in view while aiming.
  - Balance was simulated with a bot plus human timing error: a steady player makes par about 92% of tries, a shaky one about 70%.
- Sword levels: `S.swl` 1-30, `S.swx` kills toward the next (`swNeed(L)=5+2L`). `swordXP()` is called from `killEnemy()` (+1), boss deaths (+5) and duel wins (+3). Damage `swordDmg()` adds `swBonus()`=0.25 per level. Foes scale with crown pieces, not sword level (`pieces()`): +6% hp per piece (`swTough()`), +0.6% speed, +1 damage from 14 pieces; bosses and champions +5% hp per piece. So each level is a real gain (a level-3 bandit goes from 6 hits at the start to 2 by level 20). The level cap rises with crown pieces (`swCap()`: `capBase`=4 at the start, 30 at `capFullPieces`=14, about half the course); a capped sword banks one level of XP and shows a banner once per screen, and a new crown piece applies it (`swordXP(0)` after `golfFinish()`). XP is for sword skill (`TUNE.sword.xp`, `killXP()`): only kills whose last blow was the sword count (`swordMark(e,kind)` from sword, spin, combo and beam hits); a sword kill is 1, +1 if Wren hasn't been hit for 10 s (clean), +1 for a combo, spin or beam kill; a parry is 1; a hit taken costs 1; a boss is 5, +5 if beaten without a hit (`G.fightHurt`, `bossXP()`); a duel win is 3. Perks every 5 levels (`TUNE.sword.perks`, `perk(k)`, `PERKS` for the level-up banner): 5 reach (`swordBox()` +6, longer blade), 10 quick (A at `P.atk<=6` chains a new swing), 15 piercing beam at any health, 20 great whirl (bigger spin, 3x), 25 every third connected swing in a row hits double (`P.combo`, within 50 frames), 30 ember blade (+1 damage, sparks). From level 10 the swing leaves a trail: blue, gold from 20, ember at 30.
- Heart pieces: `heartPiece()` adds to `S.hpc`; at 4 it queues a container (`G.heartQ`, given when play resumes). Sources: every 5th sword level, champion duel wins, golf under par, an ace on a map basket (once per hole, `S.aceH`), wide moat chests, bounty boards.
- Ambush + duels: a Marebear won't start her charm duel during an ambush (`G.arena`/`G.needClear`). `startDuel()` saves the ambush state and its foes, and `endDuel()` restores them so clearing still opens the gates.
- Admin (Andrew only, per device): open the site with `?admin` to turn it on (stored in localStorage `wrenquest-admin`); `?admin=off` turns it off. Then the backtick key or the Admin button under the game opens `openAdmin()`: every hole (1-27) can jump to its dungeon (`enterDungeon(HD0+n)`, return point saved) or straight into its golf challenge (`startGolf(n)`, `G.golf.admin`); the last row enters the Obsidian Throne without the 27-piece check. Admin golf counts: par or better awards the crown piece and marks the hole won; over par offers A to replay or B to quit.
- Story: the Old Course premise (the Obsidian King cheated in the Crown Round; the Course turned him to glass; only an honest round breaks each seal). Cutscenes are `SCENES` (pages of [backdrop, speaker, text]); `playScene(name,after)` sets `G.mode='scene'`, `updScene()`/`drawScene()`/`drawSceneArt()` run them, A advances, Start skips. Beats: `opening` (new game), `first`/`nine`/`eighteen`/`all` at 1/9/18/27 crown pieces (`storyBeat()` after `golfFinish()`, queued in `G.sceneQ`, once each via `S.beats`), `throne` before the final boss, `ending` from `winGame()` before the win screen. `sceneEra()` darkens the overworld at 9/18/27 pieces (dusk, night, blood moon) until the game is won.
- Opening: `newGame()` hands Wren the putter. The first sink on hole 1's basket (the start screen) before the sword opens a practice hole: `startGolf(0,1)` sets `G.golf.tut`, uses `tutCourse()` (short, straight, no wind or water), shows `golfCoach()` lines over the HUD strip, never costs a heart, and B skips it from the retry screen. Finishing records `'practice'` in `S.beats` and sends Wren to the groundskeeper's cave on the same screen, who gives the sword, shield, bow and bombs. Without a sword, later sinks on hole 1 don't open its dungeon.
- HUD: hearts sit top right; the crown is the centrepiece (`drawCrownHUD()`): base = holes 1-9, band = 10-18, points = 19-27, with a large piece count. A new piece flashes for two seconds. The area name is capped at 19 characters so it clears the hearts.
- Sprites are pixel strings in `SPR`; palettes are swapped per enemy tier. The hero's floppy cap is drawn procedurally in `drawCap()`.
- Tests: `python3 tests/run.py` (or name suites: `world golf flows layout stress`). Needs Playwright (`pip install playwright && playwright install chromium`, or `CHROME=/path/to/chrome`). The runner injects `tests/hook.js` into a temp copy of the game (after the top-level `requestAnimationFrame(frame);` line), so tests can reach internals through `window.__WQ`. Nothing test-related ships.
  - world: every overworld exit connects (jumps allowed), all 27 hole dungeons, the throne and side dungeons generate with a boss, every cavern joins up
  - golf: every hole is par 3 or 4 and makeable in par by perfect play
  - flows: the hole loop (basket → dungeon → boss → golf, over par replays, third miss costs a heart, putting, par → crown + stairs → back to the basket), admin gate, ambush restore, sword level 30, heart pieces, a saved game loading where it left off
  - layout: about 30 screens drawn with worst-case values (16 hearts, 999 gems, level 30, try 12, long names); fails on any text drawn over other text or off screen. Each screen is drawn at 12 points across 48 frames so blinking text is checked lit and unlit. Widths assume Press Start 2P (1em per character). When you add or change a screen, add it here.
  - stress: random input across overworld, dungeons and caverns with no console errors
  - `fresh()` turns random duels off (`G.lastDuelT` in the future) and `waitDun()` waits for a hole drop while dismissing dialogs, so random encounters can't make flows flaky. In the game, `countingDown()` stops random duels from starting while the ground opens, a practice hole starts or a challenge resumes.
  Run the suite before every PR and put its result in the PR description. Add a flow or layout case for each new feature.
- Balance numbers live in `TUNE` at the top of the script (foes per screen, spawn shares, drop chances, sword scaling, stamina costs, golf meters and putting, charm and folk chances). Change them there, not inline.

## How changes ship

Branch + PR against `main`; merging deploys via the shared `Deploy` workflow (dibberlab-deploy on the droplet). Never push straight to `main`.
