The quest

The Obsidian King broke the Ember Crown into fourteen shards. Grab your gear from the sage's cave, clear fourteen dungeons for their shards and upgrades, then storm the Obsidian Throne in the far northeast. Talk to strangers (walk up and press attack) for quests, clues and lies. Eat strange mushrooms. Beware wandering champions. There are 13 secrets to find. Progress saves in this browser.
