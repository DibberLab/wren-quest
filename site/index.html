The quest

Long ago this land settled every quarrel with a round on the Old Course. When the Obsidian King cheated in the Crown Round, the Course turned him to black glass, and he shattered the Ember Crown and buried a piece under each of its 27 baskets. Get your gear from the old groundskeeper's cave, sink a basket to drop into its dungeon, beat its keeper, then play the hole honestly (par or better) to break the seal. The land darkens as the round goes on. Collect all 27 pieces and face the King on his throne in the far northeast. Talk to strangers (walk up and press attack) for quests, clues and lies. Eat strange mushrooms. Beware wandering champions. There are 15 secrets to find. Progress saves in this browser.
